    The weekend Suggested

      Field guide

      Liverpool

      1. 01

        Concert Square

        Ropewalks · the start

        Open-air square ringed with bars. Loud, young, rammed. First-pint energy — not where you want to be at 2am.

      2. 02

        Seel & Slater St

        2 min · the engine

        Right off Concert Square. Denser, better, proper bars. Most of the night should happen here.

      3. 03

        Bold Street

        4 min · food

        Independent restaurants. Eat here before you need to, not after. This is what buys you Saturday.

      4. 04

        Baltic Triangle

        12 min south · the cool one

        Warehouses, street food, bigger venues, later licences. Far enough out to feel found. Worth it once.

      5. 05

        Mathew Street

        8 min north · the tourist one

        Cavern Quarter. Beatles, live bands, unashamedly for visitors. Do it once, enjoy it honestly.

      6. ☀

        Albert Dock

        Daytime

        Saturday afternoon, to feel like a person again. Water, air, food, no strobes.

      • Contactless everywhereTap works basically universally. You'll barely touch cash.
      • A pint runs £5–7More in the busy squares, less in an old man's pub.
      • Rounds are standardIf someone gets you one, you're in the round. Pace accordingly.
      • Order at the barNo table service in most pubs. Go up, pay, carry it back.
      • Don't tip the bartenderNot expected. If someone's been great, "and one for yourself" is the move.
      • Restaurants ~10%Only if service isn't already on the bill — it often is.
      • Last orders ~11pmTraditional pubs. Ropewalks and the Baltic run much later.
      • Eat earlyBefore you think you need it.
      • Water between roundsTap water is free. Just ask.
      • Trains stop ~midnightAfter that it's a cab.
      • Pick a meeting spotNow, while sober, in case phones die.
