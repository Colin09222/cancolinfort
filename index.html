THE ROUTE

City centre is tiny — everything below is walkable. Rough order from early doors to last man standing.

1 · Concert Square

Ropewalks · the obvious start

Open-air square ringed with bars, big outdoor drinking yard. Loud, young, gets rammed. Perfect first-pint energy, not where you want to end up at 2am.

2 · Seel Street & Slater Street

2 min walk · the actual engine

Runs right off Concert Square. Denser, better, more variety — proper bars instead of just yards. This is where most of the night should happen.

3 · Bold Street

4 min walk · food + a breather

Independent restaurants and cafés. The correct place to eat before you need to, not after. Eating here is what buys you Saturday.

4 · Baltic Triangle

10–12 min walk south · the cool one

Converted warehouses, street food, bigger venues, later licences. Slightly out of the centre so it feels like you found it. Worth the walk once.

5 · Mathew Street

8 min walk north · the tourist one

Cavern Quarter. Beatles-themed, live bands, unashamedly for visitors. Do it once, enjoy it honestly, don't build a night around it.

Albert Dock / Waterfront

daytime

Where you go Saturday afternoon to feel like a person again. Water, air, food, no strobes.

BAR RULES

Order at the bar
No table service in most pubs. Go to the bar, pay there, carry it back.
Don't tip the bartender
Not expected and mildly odd. If someone's been great, "and one for yourself" is the local move.
Restaurants
~10% if service isn't already on the bill. Check the bill first — it's often added.
Contactless everywhere
Tap works basically universally. You barely need cash.
Rounds
Buying in rounds is standard. If someone gets you one, you're now in the round. Pace accordingly.
Last orders
Traditional pubs call it around 11pm. Late bars and clubs in Ropewalks and the Baltic run much later.

NOT DYING

Eat on Bold Street
Early, before you think you need it.
Water between rounds
Bar staff will give you tap water free. Ask.
Pints are 20% bigger
Six here is not six at home. This is the actual trap.
Trains stop early
Local rail winds down around midnight. After that it's a cab.
Stay together
Agree a meeting spot now, while sober, in case phones die.
